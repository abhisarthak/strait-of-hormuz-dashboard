INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Holm correction controls family-wise error across the 90 Script 35 paired tests.
2. Shapiro-Wilk tests assess normality of paired differences; with n=30, interpret diagnostics alongside plots rather than as a definitive model check.
3. IQR flags are descriptive potential outliers, not grounds for automatic removal.
4. Wilcoxon signed-rank tests provide a non-parametric sensitivity analysis; they test paired differences around zero under their own assumptions.
5. Confidence intervals and p-values concern simulated outcomes under the specified hypothetical recovery rules, not observed operational effects.
6. The policy rank-sum is descriptive and depends on treating delay and peak rank as equally weighted objectives.
7. Pareto efficiency is limited to the two selected model outputs.
8. Maximum port-specific peak concurrency is not berth occupancy, port capacity, or a validated congestion measure.
9. Port-call sequences do not establish continuous voyages or confirmed Strait of Hormuz crossings. AIS/equivalent movement evidence is still required.
10. Higher replication count improves Monte Carlo precision but does not correct data coverage, parameter, or structural model limitations.
