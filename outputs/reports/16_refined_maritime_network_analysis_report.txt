7. METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Schedule and Individual datasets are analyzed separately.
- The sequence-ready Schedule excludes one representative record from each of seven source-verified duplicate-representation pairs.
- The annotated full Schedule remains preserved.
- Inter-port elapsed time is not necessarily pure sailing time.
- Elapsed time may include anchorage, waiting, intermediate stops, data gaps and operational delays.
- Same-LOCODE transitions are retained and classified.
- Small transition samples should not be treated as reliable travel-time distributions.
- Port calls do not establish whether a vessel crossed the Strait of Hormuz.
- AIS trajectory data is required to verify Strait transit.
- The histogram visualization excludes its upper five percent only for display; analytical tables retain all observations.
