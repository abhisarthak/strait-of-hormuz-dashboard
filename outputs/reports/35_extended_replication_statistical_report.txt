METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The paired t-tests compare simulated policy outcomes across matched random seeds.
2. P-values are unadjusted across the multiple policy/metric comparisons; treat them as exploratory.
3. Statistical significance does not establish operational or causal validity.
4. Thirty seeds improve Monte Carlo precision but do not correct limitations in the underlying data or model.
5. Recovery fractions and fixed-hour limits are hypothetical sensitivity assumptions.
6. Port-call concurrency is not berth occupancy, verified capacity, or direct congestion.
7. No AIS-confirmed Strait of Hormuz transit is inferred.
8. Accumulated call-delay hours sum delay across affected call records, not unique-vessel delay.
