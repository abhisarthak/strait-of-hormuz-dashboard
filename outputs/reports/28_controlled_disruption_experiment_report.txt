INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Disruption percentages and delay durations are hypothetical experimental assumptions.
Affected calls are selected randomly and are not confirmed Strait of Hormuz transits.
Arrival and departure timestamps are shifted equally, preserving observed port-stay duration.
Concurrency is calculated from port-call time intervals and does not establish berth occupancy or port capacity.
This experiment does not simulate vessel rerouting, sailing-time changes, or queue formation.
Results are conditional on the selected historical schedule and experimental assumptions.
