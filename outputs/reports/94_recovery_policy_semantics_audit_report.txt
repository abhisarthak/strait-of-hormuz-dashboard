INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
- Recovery credits are hypothetical mathematical allowances, not measured capabilities.
- The Script77 elapsed-time cap uses observed inter-call elapsed time; it is not schedule slack.
- Inter-port elapsed time is not pure sailing time; no route or actual Hormuz transit is established.
- This audit does not establish operational feasibility, safety, cost, fuel, emissions, or capacity.
- Missing schema fields cause explicit failed checks; they are not silently inferred.
- No policy, estimand, censoring rule, or experiment execution is approved by this script.
- No simulation is run and no upstream input is modified.
