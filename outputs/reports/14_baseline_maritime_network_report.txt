8. INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Schedule and individual vessel-call datasets are analysed separately.
B. Actual arrival and departure timestamps are used for actual baseline analysis.
C. Negative port stays are excluded from valid actual records.
D. Inter-port elapsed time is calculated from previous actual departure to next actual arrival. It is not necessarily pure sailing time.
E. Repeated consecutive LOCODEs are retained. They may reflect terminal-level or operational call representations.
F. Port-call sequences do not prove that a vessel transited the Strait of Hormuz. AIS trajectory evidence is required.
G. The core and conservative baselines are compared as a sensitivity analysis. Candidate records are not silently deleted from the original datasets.
H. These descriptive statistics are preliminary calibration inputs, not yet validated simulation parameters.
