INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The confidence intervals are descriptive intervals across the five simulated seeds. They do not quantify uncertainty in real-world maritime performance.
2. Five seeds are a small exploratory replication set. Conclusions about policy superiority should be treated cautiously.
3. Paired comparisons use the same seed-specific vessel and disruption-onset selection as the NO_RECOVERY reference.
4. Rank sums are descriptive summaries, not validated multi-objective utility functions.
5. Pareto screening uses mean accumulated delay and mean maximum port-specific peak; it does not prove an operational optimum.
6. Port-call concurrency is not berth occupancy, port capacity, or a direct congestion measure.
7. Recovery percentages and fixed-hour limits remain hypothetical until supported by measured schedule slack or operational evidence.
8. No AIS-confirmed Strait of Hormuz transit is inferred by this analysis.
