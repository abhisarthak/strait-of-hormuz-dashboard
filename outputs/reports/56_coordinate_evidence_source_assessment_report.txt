RESEARCH LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. A populated coordinate pair is not automatically a validated coordinate.
2. Different coordinate values may represent different physical reference points.
3. Sources from the same organization are not independent organizational corroboration.
4. Different organization names indicate potential organizational independence only; they do not prove independent underlying evidence or coordinate corroboration.
5. Port names and Script 49 eligibility fields are restored from Script 54 by exact UN/LOCODE matching; missing or unmatched keys cause the script to stop.
6. Coordinate distance alone cannot establish whether two sources refer to the same facility.
7. A port reference point, port limit, terminal, berth and anchorage are distinct geographic objects.
8. Missing datum or CRS information remains a limitation.
9. Script 56 makes no coordinate approvals or operational inclusion decisions.
10. No AIS-confirmed Strait of Hormuz transit is inferred.
