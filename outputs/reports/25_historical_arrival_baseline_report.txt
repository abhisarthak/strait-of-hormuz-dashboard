METHODOLOGICAL ASSUMPTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Historical arrival timestamps are replayed as observed.
2. Observed port stay is used as a provisional occupancy duration.
3. Port capacities 1, 2 and 3 are experimental assumptions.
4. Actual port berth capacities are not established by this model.
5. Port stay is not confirmed berth-processing time.
6. Queue waiting is simulated separately from the occupancy proxy.
7. Historical inter-port elapsed times are not added again.
8. No direct sailing routes are inferred.
9. No actual Hormuz transit is confirmed.
10. Results are conditional on the selected capacity assumptions.


MODEL LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The historical port-call duration may already contain waiting, anchorage, operational and other time components. Therefore, adding simulated queue waiting can result in overlapping time components. The results must be interpreted as a provisional queueing experiment, not as validated estimates of actual port turnaround or berth utilisation.
