METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
All disruption levels and delays are hypothetical assumptions.
Affected calls are randomly selected and are not confirmed Hormuz transits.
Concurrency uses historical port-call intervals as occupancy proxies, not actual berth occupancy.
The common window is the same for every scenario and seed. It starts at the earliest historical arrival and ends at the latest scenario departure.
The model does not yet include rerouting, vessel-level voyage propagation, or queueing.
