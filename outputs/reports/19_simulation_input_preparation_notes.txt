SCRIPT 19 - SIMULATION INPUT PREPARATION
=========================================

Primary baseline: conservative schedule actual calls.
Core schedule: sensitivity dataset; kept separate.
Individual calls: comparison dataset; kept separate.

Port service time is observed arrival-to-departure duration.
It is not necessarily berth processing time.

Inter-port elapsed time is previous departure to next arrival.
It is not pure sailing time and may include waiting or unobserved calls.

No vessel capacity, port capacity, berth capacity, or sailing speed has been invented.

The 18F port validation register remains a pending review register. Its locations are not treated as independently verified ports.

Disruption levels and duration are experimental assumptions, not historical measurements.

AIS transit exposure through the Strait of Hormuz is not confirmed by these schedule records.
